Terms

Last updated: November 10, 2026 (America/New_York). These terms describe the planned packaged sale of RuntimeGate. Checkout is not live.

The product

RuntimeGate 0.1.0 is a Node.js CLI sold as a one-time $19 USD packaged release with MIT-licensed source included. You are buying a packaged release and docs convenience, not exclusive proprietary rights to the code. MIT permission includes use, modification, and redistribution subject to the license notice.

Honest limits

RuntimeGate is a consistency gate. It does not guarantee that production runs a pinned runtime. Parsing of YAML and TOML is best-effort. End-of-support data is vendored and can go stale.
